Ember Glass is a dark glass theme with warm parchment text and a copper accent, matching a Ghostty setup with `background-opacity = 0.7`. Panels are see-through and popups are frosted glass. Paseo's window is opaque by default, so the desktop only shows through after patching the app with `scripts/transparent-window.py` from this repository; without the patch the theme still works with a solid dark background.
